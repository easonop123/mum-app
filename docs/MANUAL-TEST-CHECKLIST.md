# Manual test checklist

Automated tests ran in Chromium only, at iPhone size. Check these on her actual phone, and note the date, the device and the iOS version.

## A. Real iPhone (Safari / WebKit)

- [ ] https://easonop123.github.io/mum-app/ opens in Safari, and **Let's start** goes straight to Today with **Start today's practice** available.
- [ ] Add to Home Screen works. The icon shows (an off-white dot and line on jade green). It opens without Safari controls.
- [ ] If the first version was installed before: it offers **Update now** (or updates itself on the next open), and afterwards practice is available with no "paused" message.
- [ ] Offline: visit once online, turn on Airplane Mode, relaunch from the Home Screen. Today, the movements' written steps, the plan and her history load. Videos say they need the internet.
- [ ] During a practice the screen stays on. Locking and unlocking the phone keeps the same step, with no extra reps recorded.
- [ ] **停止 / Stop → Yes, stop** saves the practice and shows the 111 / Healthline buttons. Tapping them opens the phone's call confirmation; nothing dials by itself.
- [ ] Text size: iOS Larger Text plus the app's own "Largest" setting. The Stop button and Help stay visible, and nothing is cut off.
- [ ] VoiceOver in English and in Chinese reads the Today and practice screens sensibly.
- [ ] Spoken cues: with "Mandarin 普通话" on, *Read the steps aloud* speaks Chinese. If no Mandarin voice is installed, the app says so.
- [ ] Reminders: the iPhone Reminders steps match the real labels. The calendar file imports and repeats on her practice days at the chosen time.
- [ ] Backup and restore move her history between Safari and the Home Screen app (they may keep separate data).

## B. Videos, on her phone in New Zealand

For each entry in [VIDEO-SOURCES.md](VIDEO-SOURCES.md):

- [ ] It plays inside the app, or **Open on YouTube** works.
- [ ] The movement shown matches the written steps, and an easier version isn't swapped for a harder one.
- [ ] Note the spoken language and captions (Mandarin, Cantonese, English).

## C. Chinese she can read easily

- [ ] She, or another fluent reader, reads the Chinese screens: welcome, Today, each movement, rest, Stop, Plan, Progress and Settings. The words are plain and kind, with no unexplained jargon.
