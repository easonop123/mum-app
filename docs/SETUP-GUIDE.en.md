# Setting up 一点力 · A Little Stronger (family guide)

## 1. Put it on her iPhone

1. Open **https://easonop123.github.io/mum-app/** in **Safari**. You can also point the iPhone Camera at the QR code ([open-on-iphone-qr.png](open-on-iphone-qr.png)) and tap the link.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. If you see **Open as Web App**, make sure it is on.
5. Tap **Add**.

The labels can look a little different on different iOS versions. The website also works without steps 2–5.

Open it once while online. After that, everything except the videos works offline.

**If she already installed the first version:** the app updates itself the next time she opens it. If she sees "A new version of the app is ready", tap **Update now**.

## 2. First time

She picks English or 简体中文 and taps **Let's start**. That's all. The language can be switched any time with the button at the top.

## 3. Practising

- Tap **Start today's practice**. The first screen has a short setup list (chair against the wall, hold the kitchen counter, good shoes, phone where she can see it) and an optional 2-minute walking warm-up.
- One movement at a time: watch the demonstration if she likes, then tap **Done**, **I did fewer**, or **Skip**.
- **停止 / Stop** is always on screen. It saves what she did and shows the 111 and Healthline numbers.
- Practice days are Tuesday and Friday by default. She can change them in **Settings → Practice days**, keeping a rest day between them.
- After two full practices at a stage, the app asks if she'd like a little more. **Keep this amount** is always fine. She can also pick any stage on the **Plan** page.

## 4. Reminders (optional)

The app can't send notifications. **Settings → Reminders** has steps for an iPhone Reminder, and a calendar file she can choose to add.

## 5. Backups

Everything is stored only on the phone. Every so often, use **Settings → My data → Save a backup** and keep the file somewhere private.

Use **Restore** when moving between Safari and the Home Screen app, or to a new phone.

## Help

- Emergency: **111**
- Not sure how urgent? Healthline **0800 611 116** (free, any time)
