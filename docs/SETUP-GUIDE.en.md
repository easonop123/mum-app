# Setting up 一点力 · A Little Stronger (family guide)

This app is hers to use, or not. Please let her choose. It is not a doctor, and it can't say whether exercise is safe for her.

## 1. Put it on her iPhone

1. Open **https://easonop123.github.io/mum-app/** in **Safari**. You can also point the iPhone Camera at the QR code ([open-on-iphone-qr.png](open-on-iphone-qr.png)) and tap the link.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. If you see **Open as Web App**, make sure it is on.
5. Tap **Add**.

The labels can look a little different on different iOS versions. The website also works without step 2–5.

Open it once while online. After that, the written instructions, the plan, the help numbers and her history work offline. The videos always need the internet.

## 2. First run: she answers, in her own words

- She chooses English or 简体中文. The language can be switched at any time with the button at the top.
- She confirms New Zealand, so the help numbers are 111 and Healthline 0800 611 116.
- She answers a few health questions, one at a time. **"Not sure" is a good answer.** Please don't answer for her. A family member's view is not a medical check.
- If she reports any emergency signs, the app stops and shows the help numbers. That is intended.

## 3. "Waiting for medical advice" is expected

The plan starts paused on purpose. Before practising:

1. Open **Settings → Summary for GP or physio**. It has an English version for the doctor and a Chinese version for her. Show it, print it, or copy it. Nothing is sent automatically.
2. At the appointment, ask the questions in the summary.
3. Afterwards, she records what they said in **Settings → GP or physio advice**: whether they looked at the back history, and for each movement whether it is "OK for me".
4. If the GP or physio **changed** the routine, the app cannot apply that change itself. The routine's content needs a deliberate revision.

## 4. Before the first practice

**Settings → Home setup check:**

- a solid chair against the wall
- a fixed counter to hold
- secure shoes
- clear floor space
- the phone propped where she can read it without bending

Dumbbells are **not** needed. Leave that page alone unless a physio later suggests weights.

## 5. Everyday use

- Practice days are Tuesday and Friday by default. She can change them in Settings, with at least one rest day between.
- **停止 / Stop** is always on screen during a practice.
- The day after a practice, the app asks how she felt the next time she opens it.
- Moving up a stage only ever happens if she chooses it. "Keep this amount / 保持这个量" is always fine.

## 6. Reminders (optional)

The app can't send notifications. **Settings → Reminders** has steps for an iPhone Reminder, and a calendar file she can choose to add.

## 7. Backups

Everything is stored only on the phone. Every so often, use **Settings → My data → Save a backup** and keep the file somewhere private.

Use **Restore** when moving between Safari and the Home Screen app, or to a new phone. After a restore she will be asked the health questions again.

## Help

- Emergency: **111**
- Not sure how urgent? Healthline **0800 611 116** (free, any time)
